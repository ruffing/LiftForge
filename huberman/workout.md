## Week 1

### Sunday — Long Endurance (Zone 2)

1. Zone 2 cardio: jogging, rowing, cycling, swimming, or hiking. Breathe through your nose when you can. — **45 min**

### Monday — Legs

1. Barbell squats — **4x6**
2. Leg extensions — **2x8**
3. Deadlift — **4x6**
4. Lying leg curls — **2x8**
5. Standing calf raises — **3x8**

### Tuesday — Core + Heat & Cold Recovery

1. Leg raises — **3x12**
2. Weighted Russian twists — **3x10 each side**
3. Plank — **3x30 sec**
4. Hand walkouts — **3x6**
5. Sauna (20 min) followed by an ice bath or cold shower (5 min) — **2 rounds**

### Wednesday — Torso & Neck

1. Bench press — **4x6**
2. Dumbbell flyes — **2x10**
3. Pull-ups — **4x max**
4. Seated cable rows — **2x8**
5. Standing overhead press — **4x6**
6. Dumbbell lateral raises — **2x10**
7. Neck curls (lying, plate on forehead) — **2x15**
8. Lateral neck raises — **2x15**

### Thursday — Cardio + Core

1. Moderate-intensity cardio at 75–80% effort: running, rowing, cycling, stair climbing, or jump rope, outdoors if possible — **35 min**
2. Crunch machine — **3x12**
3. Spiderman plank — **3x10 sec each side**
4. Side plank — **3x30 sec each side**
5. Suitcase carry — **3x30 m each side**

### Friday — HIIT

1. All-out sprint for 20 sec, then 10 sec rest, on an assault bike, rower, ski machine, or running sprints — **8 rounds**

### Saturday — Arms, Neck & Calves

1. Incline dumbbell curls — **4x6**
2. Standing barbell curls — **2x8**
3. Cable triceps pushdowns — **4x6**
4. Overhead dumbbell triceps extensions — **2x8**
5. Seated calf raises — **3x12**
6. Tibialis raises (wall) — **2x20**
7. Neck extensions (harness or plate) — **2x15**
8. 4-way band neck isometrics — **2x15 sec each direction**
